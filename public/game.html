Ludo Rules & Shortcuts

Roll a 6: Moves a pawn from your yard onto the starting tile, and grants an extra roll.
Captures: Landing on an opponent sends their pawn back to base and awards you a bonus turn.
Safe Squares: Tiles marked with stars are completely safe from captures.
Victory: First player to guide all four pawns home wins the arena!
Shortcuts: Press Space to roll dice, and 1, 2, 3, 4 to choose pawns.
